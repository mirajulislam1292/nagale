# Nagale pitch notes

Small jobs in Bangladesh are frequently agreed through scattered conversations. A shared record, clear scope and protected payment can reduce uncertainty for both parties.

The initial wedge is small digital and locally coordinated tasks: CVs, shop websites, tuition, photography and document help. The product loop is describe → match → agree → escrow → deliver → review → payout. Reputation, verified skills and portfolio history make repeat work easier. The provider fee is supplied by configuration; the sample configuration is 10%.

## What exists

The current branch adds a one-screen login Gate with an interactive deterministic brief example and an editable EN/BN About story. Existing marketplace screens provide mock proposals, order actions, messaging, wallet and review tools. They are prototype interfaces, not operational payment or identity services.

The new final-master home feeds, social posts, saved work, notifications, complete route guards and complete language migration remain in progress. Do not claim all final-master routes or verification targets are complete.

## Backend before launch

Authenticated sessions, authorisation, server-side masking and idempotency, persistent jobs/messages/reviews, secure verification uploads, real payment settlement and payout reconciliation, notifications, moderation audit trails and retention rules.

## Three-minute demo script

1. Open the Gate; choose a task example and review budget and deadline.
2. Choose a sample proposal; inspect the held-payment example.
3. Continue through the phone/OTP prototype (123456 in demo mode).
4. Carry the request into the existing client intake and confirm the brief.
5. Open proposals and compare price and delivery time.
6. Accept as client and provider; fund the mock order.
7. Submit as provider, inspect the watermarked preview as client and approve.
8. Switch to admin review; explain the remaining backend and moderation work.

## Assumptions to verify

Which first categories have repeat demand; whether both sides accept the holding and fee model; verification friction; dispute evidence requirements; payment-provider capabilities; distribution and retention. No market size, adoption or revenue numbers are asserted.
